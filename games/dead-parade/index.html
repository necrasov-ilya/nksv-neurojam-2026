01 / LEAD

WASD move · mouse look · SHIFT sprint · SPACE dash. Your leader bites nearby humans automatically.

02 / MULTIPLY

Infect humans to grow your horde. Athletes become runners; heavy civilians become brutes. Overrun shelters for reinforcements. Your followers attack nearby threats.

03 / UNLEASH

Q surges the horde. 1–4 select a mutation; E unleashes its special attack. Dash out of grenade rings and break the gun lines.

04 / CONQUER

STORY: meet each objective and follow the EXIT marker. Overrun all five districts to win. Collect XP for 38 ranked upgrades; click a card or press 1–3. TAB checks your build; ESC pauses.

05 / SPREAD FOREVER

ENDLESS: overrun the population, open shelters and push through the exit into another procedural sector. Ten new biomes span winter, spring, summer and autumn. Each new defense tier brings tougher resistance; ELITE sectors reinforce the line. There is no final evacuation—survive as far as you can.

06 / READ THE STREETS

Rain, snow, heat haze, fog and ash change the atmosphere and visibility, not movement or damage. Watch sniper aim, dodge explosive markers and swarm shooters while they reload. A restart keeps your current story or endless mode.
